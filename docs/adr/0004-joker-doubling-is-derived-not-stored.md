# Joker doubling is derived when points are summed, not stored on answers

A team's answers to its joker round count double, but an answer's stored `points_awarded` stays the question's points: every total (standings, round points, the scoresheet, session result, the league table) doubles the joker round's points from the team's joker round as it sums. Storing doubled points on the answer would keep totals plain sums, but a team's joker can change after it has answered (the quiz master fixes it, a deleted round is redrawn, a reopened joker pick clears a draw), so every change would mean rewriting answers, and grading (half points, margins, the key-fix check that tells an automatic grade from a moderator's) would have to know about jokers to undo the doubling. Deriving it keeps grading joker-free and a joker change a one-row write, at the price that every place that sums points must apply the joker, so they all go through one shared step. Expect `sum(points_awarded)` not to match a team's total in a session played with jokers.
