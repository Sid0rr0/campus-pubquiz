# Early answer reveal has its own statuses, not a setting-dependent `reveal`

With the Early answer reveal session setting on, a block's correct answers air between its lock and its break, before grading is done. That walk gets its own statuses (`answers_intro`, `answers`) in the **revealing** group but not in **graded**, rather than reusing `reveal_intro`/`reveal`. Reusing them would have made "graded" depend on a session setting, yet the status groups exist so that the phones, the big screen and the backend answer "can scores be trusted?" from the status alone and can never disagree. Two more statuses are a fixed cost; a group that depends on a setting would have to be remembered at every call site. With the setting on, the post-break `reveal` walk doesn't run: leaving the break goes to the next block's `round_intro` (or `ended`) with the leaderboard up, and the phones show their verdicts underneath it.
